2. The First 30 Seconds (The Hook)
Start in the action: Skip lengthy backstories or warm-up paragraphs. Open with a vivid anecdote, a surprising metric, or a provocative question.   
Medium

Frame around the reader: Move from "I" to "You" quickly. Explain early on why your experience or analysis matters to the person reading it.

3. Formatting & Mobile Skimmability
Over 60% of Medium traffic comes from mobile screens. Long, dense blocks of text will cause readers to drop off quickly.

Keep paragraphs under 3–4 lines: Frequent line breaks create natural visual pacing.

Use section headers (H2 & H3): Guide readers through your main takeaways using clear subheadings.   
Medium

Leverage visual breaks: Use quote blocks (>), bullet lists, and horizontal dividers to keep the layout dynamic.
